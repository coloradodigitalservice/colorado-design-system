# Security and Responsible Disclosure Policy

The Colorado Digital Service is committed to ensuring the security of the Colorado by protecting their information from unwarranted disclosure. We want security researchers to feel comfortable reporting vulnerabilities they have discovered so we can fix them and keep our users safe. We developed our disclosure policy to reflect our values and uphold our sense of responsibility to security researchers who share their expertise with us in good faith.

*Submit a vulnerability:* Vulnerability reports can be submitted through email to infosec@state.co.us. 

Review the [State of Colorado Disclosure Policy and scope:
](https://oit.colorado.gov/standards-policies-guides/guide-to-cybersecurity/office-of-information-security).
