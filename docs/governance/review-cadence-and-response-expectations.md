# CoDS Review Cadence and Response Expectations

**Status:** Draft for G0 approval  
**Related backlog item:** [CODS-P0-001](../backlog/phase-0/CODS-P0-001-establish-ownership-and-decision-rights.md)

## Cadence

- **Weekly delivery review:** The Aten delivery lead, State product owners, and relevant domain roles review progress, risks, dependencies, decisions, and gate readiness. The backlog, risk register, and decision log are updated after the meeting.
- **Asynchronous review:** Review requests identify the required reviewer, requested response, affected gate, and due date.
- **Gate review:** Gate evidence is circulated at least two business days before the scheduled review. Required approvers record approve, approve with conditions, hold, or replan; unresolved conditions receive an owner and due date.
- **Urgent escalation:** Security, privacy, production, accessibility-blocking, or schedule-critical issues are routed immediately to the relevant accountable role and State product owners.

## Response expectations

- Standard asynchronous review requests receive a response within two business days.
- Urgent security, privacy, production, accessibility-blocking, or schedule-critical issues are acknowledged within one business day.
- Reviewers respond with **approve**, **approve with conditions**, **request changes**, or **identify a decision request**.
- Lack of response does not constitute approval. The Aten delivery lead escalates overdue reviews at the next weekly review or sooner when the gate or release is at risk.
- Gate evidence and approval conditions remain linked to the decision log and affected backlog items.
